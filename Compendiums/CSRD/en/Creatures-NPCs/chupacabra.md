## Chupacabra

Part huge dog, part crocodile; quilled like a porcupine. Chupacabras are mysterious predators that drain the blood of livestock and sometimes people.

**Motive:** Hungers for blood  
**Environment:** Almost anywhere, but prefers rural areas  
**Health:** 13  
**Damage Inflicted:** 5 points  
**Armor:** 2  
**Movement:** Short  
**Modifications:** Speed defense as level 4 due to quickness; stealth and perception as level 7

### Combat

Chupacabras bite for 5 points of damage. Long‑range quill attack puts target to sleep for one hour on a failed difficulty 5 Might defense roll (target can try to wake each round).
