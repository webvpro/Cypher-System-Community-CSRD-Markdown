## Deathtumbler

Human‑sized tumbleweeds whose "thorns" are actually animal fangs. These ambulatory plants roll across the landscape, feeding on whatever they can catch.

**Motive:** Feed on carrion and living flesh  
**Environment:** Desert and plains  
**Health:** 9  
**Damage Inflicted:** 3 points  
**Movement:** Short  
**Modifications:** Speed defense as level 4; disguise (as rotting carrion) as level 6

### Combat

Slashes with toothed branches for 3 points of damage. Mundane attacks deal only 1 point of damage each. Attacks all targets in immediate area if it has surprise, dealing damage and 3 additional points of Speed damage (ignores Armor) on a failed Might defense roll.
