## Locomotem

20‑foot (6 m) tall humanoid figure fabricated from a coal‑fed train engine. Steam and smoke constantly pour from its joints and riveted seams.

**Motive:** Follow creator's commands (but actually break free)  
**Environment:** Industrial areas and rail yards  
**Health:** 30  
**Damage Inflicted:** 7 points  
**Armor:** 5  
**Movement:** Short  
**Modifications:** Speed defense as level 4 due to size

### Combat

Attacks with massive fists for 7 points of damage. Steam whistle (once per hour) stuns creatures in immediate range on failed Might defense roll. Has standard forgeborn traits.
