## Ghost Rider

Harbingers of chaos and catastrophe, ghost riders spread havoc long past their deaths. They ride skeletal mounts that leave burning hoofprints.

**Motive:** Spread chaos and catastrophe  
**Environment:** Anywhere, riding skeletal mounts  
**Health:** 20  
**Damage Inflicted:** 6 points  
**Armor:** 1  
**Movement:** Long (mounted)

### Combat

Rides a skeletal mount a long distance each round. Long‑range pistol attack deals 6 damage. Short‑range curse causes target who fails an Intellect defense roll to lose their next turn as they freeze in terror.
