## Nightcrawler

20‑foot (6 m) long earthworm with skin stripped from past prey. These horrific predators tunnel through earth and emerge to drag victims underground.

**Motive:** Feed on flesh  
**Environment:** Underground tunnels and caves  
**Health:** 12  
**Damage Inflicted:** 5 points  
**Movement:** Short; burrows  
**Modifications:** Speed defense as level 3

### Combat

Bite deals 5 points of damage. Can burrow to avoid attacks and emerge unexpectedly.
