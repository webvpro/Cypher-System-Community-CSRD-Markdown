## Hellfire Steed
Intermittently ridden straight out of Hell by demons wearing borrowed
flesh, the Damned, and others seeking to escape the accursed underworld,
hellfire steeds are demonic, fire‑dripping destriers. Their manes are
boiling napalm, their glowing red hooves leave prints that burn and
smolder for days, and a single demonic horn curls from their brows. Once
free of Hell, these steeds typically buck their riders and race off
across the prairielands, setting blazes as they go, apparently seeking
to wreak as much havoc as they can before they're reclaimed by Hell.
