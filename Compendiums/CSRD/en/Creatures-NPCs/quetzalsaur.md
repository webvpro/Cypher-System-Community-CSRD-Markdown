## Quetzalsaur

Giraffe‑sized monstrosity with a 40‑foot (12 m) wingspan. These ancient predators combine the worst aspects of pterosaur and necromancer.

**Motive:** Territorial predator  
**Environment:** Mountain peaks and high plateaus  
**Health:** 33  
**Damage Inflicted:** 7 points  
**Armor:** 2  
**Movement:** Long when flying  
**Modifications:** Speed defense as level 4 due to size

### Combat

Flies a long distance each round. Beak attack for 7 points of damage. Vomits up two zombie helpers (corpses of past victims) once per conflict.
