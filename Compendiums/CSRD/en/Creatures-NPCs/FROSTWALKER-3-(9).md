## Frostwalker
A frostwalker is a cursed beast made of packed snow layered over the
severed limbs, heads, gristle, antlers, and sinew from deer, wolves, and
sometimes people who've died in the cold. As such, they vary in
appearance. Some are about the size of wolves, others are human‑sized
with antlers, and a few---especially those with more limbs and/or heads
than normal---can be up to twice the size of a person.
Frostwalkers may be creations of dire sorcery, though some gain motive
force from the spirits of blizzards, ice, and enmity earned through past
atrocity.
