## Zombie Sorcerer

Magic‑infused corpse that knows enough sorcery to be dangerous. Dark energy crackles around their rotting form.

**Motive:** Spread undeath and decay  
**Environment:** Graveyards and cursed places  
**Health:** 18  
**Damage Inflicted:** 4 points Speed damage  
**Movement:** Immediate

### Combat

Long‑range necrotic blast deals 4 points of Speed damage (ignores Armor) to all in an immediate area, or 1 point even with a successful defense roll. Even‑numbered attack rolls that would reduce its health to 0 reduce health to 1 instead.
