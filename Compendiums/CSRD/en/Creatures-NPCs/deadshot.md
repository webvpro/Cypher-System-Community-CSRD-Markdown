## Deadshot

Eight‑foot (3 m) figure stitched together from the reanimated flesh of deceased gunslingers. Multiple arms allow for incredible shooting speed.

**Motive:** Follow creator's commands  
**Environment:** Battlefields and boot hills  
**Health:** 27  
**Damage Inflicted:** 7 points  
**Armor:** 4  
**Movement:** Short  
**Modifications:** Initiative as level 8

### Combat

Long‑range pistol attack deals 7 points of damage. Once a minute fires necrotic bullet that deals 3 points of damage each round (ignores Armor) until bullet extracted with a difficulty 4 healing task.
