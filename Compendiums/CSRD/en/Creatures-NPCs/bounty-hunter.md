## Bounty Hunter

Figure in sleek, form‑fitting dark duster and wide‑brimmed hat with twin guns. These professional hunters track their quarry with relentless determination.

**Motive:** Collect bounties and track targets  
**Environment:** Frontier towns and wilderness  
**Health:** 15  
**Damage Inflicted:** 4 points  
**Armor:** 1  
**Movement:** Short  
**Modifications:** Tracking, surveillance, stealth, and disguise as level 6

### Combat

Attacks twice with long‑range pistol. A melee attack with locking cuffs (level 5) binds target to a nearby large object on a failed Speed defense roll.
