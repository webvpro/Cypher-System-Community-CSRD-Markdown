## Dire Coyote
Remnants of ancient populations reinvigorated by cursed magic, dire
coyotes hunt lost and isolated creatures, regardless of whether the prey
is alive or already dead. Dire coyotes are gaunt and sinewy, almost as
large as a horse. Their hides are branded with arcane sigils that still
glow and smoke with the sorcery that pulses through them.
Dire coyotes usually hunt in teams of two or three. However, their howls
give the impression of a far larger number of individuals in the night.
