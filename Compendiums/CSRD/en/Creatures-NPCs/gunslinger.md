## Gunslinger

Grim‑faced pistoleer pursuing vengeance, doing crime, or making a name for themselves. Their reputation precedes them into every saloon and street.

**Motive:** Vengeance, crime, or reputation  
**Environment:** Frontier towns and saloons  
**Health:** 18  
**Damage Inflicted:** 6 points (8 if attacking first)  
**Armor:** 1  
**Movement:** Short  
**Modifications:** Initiative as level 7

### Combat

Long‑range pistol attack deals 6 points of damage (or 8 points with eased attack if they attack before their foe). Every hit increases damage by 1 point for the rest of the encounter (to a maximum of +10).
