## Wraith Lord

A malign artifact or especially powerful curse may form an exceptional wraith. These are the generals of the undead legions.

**Motive:** Malign dominion over the living  
**Environment:** Haunted locations and battlefields  
**Health:** 22  
**Damage Inflicted:** 6 points Speed damage  
**Armor:** 1  
**Movement:** Short when flying  
**Modifications:** Stealth as level 6

### Combat

Flies a short distance each round. Spectral weapon deals 6 points of Speed damage (ignores Armor) and induces target to fall one step on the damage track each day a Might defense roll fails (three successes purge the effect). If destroyed, usually reforms the following midnight.
