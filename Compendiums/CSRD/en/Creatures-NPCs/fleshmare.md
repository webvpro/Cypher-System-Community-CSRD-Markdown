## Fleshmare

Made from the stitched‑together flesh of deceased horses. These grotesque constructs retain the speed and power of their component parts.

**Motive:** Follow creator's commands (but actually break free)  
**Environment:** Stables and abandoned ranches  
**Health:** 24  
**Damage Inflicted:** 6 points  
**Armor:** 3  
**Movement:** Short

### Combat

Hoof attack deals 6 points of damage. Electrical damage heals instead of harms fleshmare. Has standard forgeborn traits.
