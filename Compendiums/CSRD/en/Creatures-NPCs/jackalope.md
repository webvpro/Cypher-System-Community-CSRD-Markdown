## Jackalope

Jackrabbit with the horns of an antelope. These trickster creatures use illusions to confuse and mislead travelers.

**Motive:** Mischief and territory defense  
**Environment:** Plains and desert  
**Health:** 6  
**Damage Inflicted:** 3 points  
**Movement:** Long; immediate when burrowing  
**Modifications:** Speed defense as level 4; stealth and illusions as level 5

### Combat

Gores with antlers for 3 points of damage. Creates illusion that can fill a short area and lasts for ten minutes or until interacted with.
