## Learned a Few Things Over Drinks  
  
>**Action**

You make a melee attack that inflicts no damage. Instead, if the attack hits, make a second Might‑based roll. If successful, a foe of level 3 or lower is knocked unconscious for one minute. For each level of Effort used, you can affect one higher level of foe, or you can extend the duration for an additional minute. If you use a whiskey bottle to make the attack, your attack is eased. If you have a similar sort of knockout ability from another source (such as your type), the attack is eased by an additional step. Action.
