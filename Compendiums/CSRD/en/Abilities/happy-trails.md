## Happy Trails
  
>**Enabler**  
  
Whether you’re traveling along a path you’ve previously marked, or you’re navigating and blazing a new trail, roaming with you puts your companions at ease. During rests on any day you’ve traveled together, you and your party gain +1 to your recovery rolls. Enabler.
