## Winning Hand
>**Enabler**
>4 Intellect points   

If you win a hand of cards or other wager, or if you succeed on an Intellect defense roll to resist a foe’s attack, you gain an action. You can use it immediately even if you have already taken a turn in the round. You can use this ability once per round. Enabler.
