## Never Miss a Shot
  
>**Enabler**  

If you shoot at a target and miss, reroll. If you hit, it’s because your shot ricochets off a nearby surface and actually hits, inflicting 2 fewer points of damage. Each time you reroll the same shot, 2 more points are deducted from the total damage, until the wildly ricocheting shot finally hits its target or the damage amount goes to 0. Enabler.
