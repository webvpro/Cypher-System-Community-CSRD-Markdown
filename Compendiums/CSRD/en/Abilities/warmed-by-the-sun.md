## Snake Charmer
>**Action**

After spending ten minutes in the sun, a hot spring, or other such warm location, you gain a free ten‑minute recovery roll. Once you use this ability, you can’t use it again until after your next ten‑hour recovery roll. Ten minutes to activate.
