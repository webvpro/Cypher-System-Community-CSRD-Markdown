## Trail Awareness
  
>**Enabler**  
  
Your connection with trails you’ve blazed is always with you. When traveling on, camping near, or within visual range of any trail you’ve blazed, you gain an asset on one attack or defense roll thanks to your knowledge about what else is using the path. Once used, this ability renews after your next recovery roll. Enabler.
