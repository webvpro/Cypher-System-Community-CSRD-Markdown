## Bonus Cypher App
>**Enabler**

You can bear one additional app cypher (your cypher limit increases by one, but only if the extra cypher is an app cypher). When you advance to a tier where you can select Expert Cypher Use, Adroit Cypher Use, or Master Cypher Use, you can replace this ability with the cypher ability you qualify for.
