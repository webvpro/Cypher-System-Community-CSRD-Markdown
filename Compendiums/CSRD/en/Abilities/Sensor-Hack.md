## Sensor Hack
>**Cost:** 4 Intellect points
>
>**Action to create; action to check**

You establish a connection with a camera-enabled device within short range, such as a security camera, smart TV or game console camera, optical cyberware of a creature (living or dead), or a mobile phone. At any time during the next 24 hours, you can see through this camera, no matter how far you move from it. (If the device also has a microphone, you can hear through that when you look through it.) Action to create; action to check.

Obviously, Sensor Hack is a hack, meaning that your connection with the device can be detected and counter-hacked, although it's much harder to do so when you're not actively using the camera.
