## SHITS Hack
>**Cost:** 7 Intellect points
>
>**Action to initiate**

A foe within short range temporarily experiences SHITS and spends its next turn attacking the nearest creature (if multiple creatures are the same distance away, you can choose which one they attack). As long as you continue to use your action each turn to maintain this berserk state in the target, they continue to attack the nearest creature (switching to a new creature if their current target drops). This effect ends when you stop using your action to control the foe, or if they are out of short range. Action to initiate.
