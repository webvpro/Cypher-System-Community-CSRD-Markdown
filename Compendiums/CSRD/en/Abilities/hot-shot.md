## Hot Shot
  
>**Enabler**  

When you hit a target with a gun attack while Smoldering Gun is active, you can choose to reduce the damage by 1 point but gain an additional related effect and/or hit a target in a precise spot. Possible effects include (but are not limited to) the following: You can ignite a structure (if you succeed on an Intellect‑based task against its level).
    
1. You can create a flaming message in the sky consisting of a handful of words that lasts for up to a minute.
    
2. You can shoot an object out of someone’s hand.
    
3. You can shoot the leg, wing, or other limb your target uses to move, reducing their maximum movement speed to immediate for a few days or until they receive expert medical care.
    
4. You can shoot a strap holding a backpack, armor, or a similarly strapped‑on item so that it falls off.
    

Enabler.
