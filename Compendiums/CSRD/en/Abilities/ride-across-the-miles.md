## Ride Across the Miles
>**Action**
    
You can ride your mount to an open location on the planet that you’re familiar with almost instantaneously. If you apply a level of Effort, you can attempt to penetrate covered locations that you’re aware of as long as a route exists from the open air to the area you want to reach. Action.
