## Corpse Datadump
>**Cost:** 3 Intellect points
>
>**Action**

You touch the remains of a recently killed creature and make an Intellect-based roll to read data from its brain cybernetics. The GM sets the difficulty based on the amount of time that has passed since the creature died. A creature that has been dead for only a few minutes is a difficulty 2 task, one that has been dead for an hour is a difficulty 4 task, and one that has been dead for a few days is a difficulty 9 task. If you succeed, you can browse the corpse's memories in a virtual environment as if you were speaking to its living self. This lasts about one minute, after which time the environment degrades. The creature is limited to what it knew in life, though it cannot recall minor memories, only big events of importance to it. When the effect ends, or if you fail the roll, the creature's memory data becomes corrupted and you can gain no further information from it. Action.
