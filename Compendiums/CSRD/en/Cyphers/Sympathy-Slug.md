## Sympathy Slug
>[!info] Stats
> **Level:** 1d6 + 1
> **Form:** "SYMPATHY" scribed on bullet case
> **Kind:** Weird West

Firing the round causes the gun to emit a spray of pleasingly
colored lights, sounds, and scents filling a short area for one hour per
cypher level. All positive interaction tasks made in the area gain an
asset. Creatures who've made up their minds about a topic may be open to
revisiting the issue. PCs add +1 to recovery rolls made in the area.
