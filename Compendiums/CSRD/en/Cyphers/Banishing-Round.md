## Banishing Round
>[!info] Stats
> **Level:** 1d6 + 1
> **Form:** "EXCLUDE LIFE" scribed on bullet case
> **Kind:** Weird West

When this round is fired from a gun, additional attacks made
from the gun gain the same benefit for the next minute. Undead, ghosts,
cursed beasts, and other supernatural targets take full damage from this
attack (if successful). Affected targets gain no benefit from Armor,
intangibility, or other protection, and lose any automatic healing
ability for a number of minutes equal to this cypher's level. Normal
targets with protective alchemical spells similarly lose that
protection. If the cypher's level is 6 or higher, successful attacks
deal +1 point of damage.
