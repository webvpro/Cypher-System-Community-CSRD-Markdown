## Fastest Keys
>[!info] Stats
> **Level:** 1d6
> **Kind:** Cyberpunk

For ten minutes per cypher level, the user's speed increases for
computer work (including hacking), allowing them to reduce the time of
any computer-related task to half the normal amount. Note that this only
affects the user's speed, not the computer's speed, so some tasks (such
as downloading a very large file) are not sped up. While the character
is netrunning, their movement is doubled and their Speed-based attacks
and Speed defense are eased.
