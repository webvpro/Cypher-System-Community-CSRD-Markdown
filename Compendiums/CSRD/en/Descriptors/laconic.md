## Laconic  
You’re a soul of few words—or no words at all, when a shrug or nod will do. Now there’s some who’ll flap their

jaws about everything that comes into their head. Sometimes they get under your skin. But you don’t upbraid

them; nope—you just frown. You’re direct and to the point, avoiding elaboration. You’re comfortable with pauses, and when you do speak, your words carry extra weight. All that said—and that’s a hell of a lot more than you would say—you don’t mind other folks. Just their prattle.
### You gain the following characteristics  
> #### Deep Waters
> +2 to your Intellect Pool. 

> #### Skill
> You’re not easily distracted. You’re trained in perception tasks.

> #### Skill
>  You’ve got a dry wit. You’re trained in positive interaction tasks.
