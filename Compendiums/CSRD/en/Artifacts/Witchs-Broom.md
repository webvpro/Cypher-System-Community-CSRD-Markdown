## Witch’s Broom
>[!info] Stats
> **Level:** 1d6 + 2
> **Form:** Long wooden broom
> **Effect:** As a flying vehicle, the broom can be ridden a long distance each round. On extended trips, it can move up to 100 miles (160 km) per hour. The bearer can call upon the broom to grant them a powerful hallucinogenic state that lasts for four hours, during which time all tasks are hindered. After the hallucinations end, the bearer’s Intellect tasks are eased for the next ten minutes.
> **Depletion:** 1 in 1d20
