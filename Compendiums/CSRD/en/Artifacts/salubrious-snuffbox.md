## Salubrious Snuffbox
  
> **Level:** 1d6 + 2  
  
> **Form:** Small ornate container of powdered tobacco (snuff)
  
> **Depletion:** 1 in 1d10  
  
> **Kind:** Artifact
  
  
  
> **Effect:** Owner who takes a pinch of snuff regains a number of Pool points of their choice equal to the artifact's level.
