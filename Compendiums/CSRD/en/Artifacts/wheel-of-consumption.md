## Wheel of Consumption
  
> **Level:** 1d6 + 2  
  
> **Form:** Crystal hoop about 1 foot (30 cm) in diameter
> **Effect Type:** Alien
  
> **Depletion:** 1 in 1d10 (upon depletion, recharged if brought into a proscribed zone)  
  
> **Kind:** Artifact
  
  
  
> **Effect:** Anything passed through the hoop whose level is less than the artifact's is disintegrated. Living creatures take damage equal to the artifact's level each round they persist in keeping any part of their body within the ambit of the wheel. This item could be used as a weapon, but it's awkward, hindering melee attacks (or a ranged attack) made with it by two steps. On a hit, the hoop deals damage equal to its level.
